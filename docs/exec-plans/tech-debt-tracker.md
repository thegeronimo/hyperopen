- Debt summary: `portfolioMargin` accounts are normalized to `:unified` in `/hyperopen/src/hyperopen/api/endpoints/account/portfolio.cljs`, so they render the Unified Account Summary. Hyperliquid treats portfolio margin as a distinct third mode with its own panel ("Portfolio Margin Summary"), its own leverage definition ("Portfolio Account Leverage = Total Cross Positions Value / LTV-adjusted Portfolio Value", where the denominator additionally includes non-collateral spot holdings times their loan-to-value factor), a ratio read directly off `spotClearinghouseState.portfolioMarginRatio` rather than computed client-side, and an extra "Borrow Cap Used" row.
  Owner team: Platform
  Impact: A portfolio-margin account sees a leverage denominator that omits its LTV-eligible collateral, a ratio computed from the wrong inputs, and labels naming a mode it is not in. The figures will disagree with the venue in the same way unified figures did before 2026-08-21.
  Retirement criteria: A distinct `:portfolio-margin` account mode carried through `[:account :mode]`, a portfolio-margin panel with the venue's labels and rows, the LTV-adjusted denominator sourced from the venue's `supplies` payload, and the ratio read from `portfolioMarginRatio`. Verified live against a real portfolio-margin wallet side by side with `app.hyperliquid.xyz`, with `npm run check`, `npm test` and `npm run test:websocket` green.
  Tracking reference: `/hyperopen/docs/exec-plans/completed/2026-08-21-unified-account-summary-hl-parity.md` (Surprises & Discoveries)
